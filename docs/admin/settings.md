# Settings

The Settings page controls the installation-wide behavior of PA.

## System

Configure business identity, application domain, logo, timezone, contact information, and outgoing email. The **Business / Organization Name** is used as the normal email sender display name unless an SMTP From Name override is set.

## Business Units

Use **Business > Business units & divisions** to model branches, regions, departments, or crews. Add existing PA users as Members or Heads and choose one primary Unit per user. These are organizational labels: they do not grant PA permissions, workforce review scope, or external-application access. A Project can use a Unit for operational context and default it from the selected Project Manager's primary Unit.

## Billing

Configure accepted payment methods, Stripe keys, webhook secret, net terms, surcharge preferences, payment receipts, and payment import behavior.

## Documents

Set standard terms, document field visibility, document custom fields, quote-to-contract automation, invoice email behavior, and public-link lifetime rules.

## Links

Enable link resolver features only if your business delivers files or external links with invoices and projects. Organization and department link behavior is managed here and on organization/project pages.

## Taxes

Create manual tax rates or import official state tax rate, FIPS, and boundary files.

## Notifications

Control cron, contract alerts, public-link warnings, onboarding notifications, invoice paid notifications, and reminder emails.

## Backup

Use the backup page to verify that backups exist, review retention, and download or restore according to your recovery policy.
