# Quotes, Contracts, and Invoices

PA supports direct document creation and quote-led workflows.

## Regular Flow

```text
Quote -> Contract -> Invoice -> Payment
```

- A quote proposes the work.
- Approval can create a contract and draft invoice.
- A signed contract activates the work.
- The invoice is finalized and sent.
- Payment moves the invoice to partial or paid.

## Long-Term Flow

Long-term contracts are for recurring services such as monthly or yearly hosting.

```text
Contract active -> Scheduled invoice -> Payment -> Next scheduled invoice
```

When a long-term contract is activated and already due, PA attempts to generate the first invoice immediately. Future invoices are generated by the cron service.

## On-Demand Flow

On-demand contracts are active agreements where each invoice is generated manually when work exists.

```text
Contract active -> Generate invoice when needed
```

## Public Document Links

Clients can use public document links to approve or deny quotes, upload signed contracts, view invoices, and pay balances. Completed outcomes remain visible briefly as status pages so clients can see that a document was approved, denied, signed, uploaded, or paid.

## Document Status Visibility

Document detail pages show the current public-link state so PA users can tell whether a client link is accessible, redirected, or unavailable.
