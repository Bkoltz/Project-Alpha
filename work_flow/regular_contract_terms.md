# LTDS Contract Terms — Regular (One-Time Projects)

> Source: Provided by Beau Koltz, June 2026. These are the default terms for
> regular one-time drone service contracts (photos/video).

## Default Terms

1. **Agreement**: This agreement is entered into between Ledge Top Drone Services (LTDS), a Sole Proprietorship organized and existing under the laws of Wisconsin with its principal place of business at 7039 County Road PP and the person(s) or business signed below.

2. **Completion**: Project completion is defined as when photos/videos are provided to Client, or 7 days have passed since Client has been notified of such completion. Interest in the amount of 18% will be applied if not paid within 30 days.

3. **Delays**: If circumstances such as weather, illness, government intervention, or other activities beyond the control of LTDS prevent the completion date from being met, Client may ask for a refund of services or authorize an extension date. An extension date must be agreed upon in any form of communication other than verbal.

4. **Liability**: Under no circumstances shall LTDS be responsible for any costs other than a Client refund if the completion date is not met for any reason.

5. **Ownership**: All Photos and Videos taken by Ledge Top Drone Services remain the property of Ledge Top Drone Services until the Client has paid the agreement in full. Client may not use any provided photos/videos for any purpose until agreement is paid in full.

6. **Property Rights**: Client states they have the legal right to have photos or video taken of described property and shall be solely responsible for all legal costs and issues of images captured for property that is unauthorized.

7. **Governing Law**: This Contract shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Wisconsin. Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be resolved through the laws governing the State of Wisconsin. Client shall be responsible for all legal costs if a breach occurs.

8. **Amendment**: No amendment or waiver of any provision of this agreement shall be valid unless provided in writing and signed by both parties.

9. **Severability**: If any provision of this agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

10. **Marketing Use**: LTDS will be able to use content captured from this project to promote their capabilities for marketing purposes.

11. **Site Security**: Unless otherwise specified, Client is solely responsible for all aspects of site security and for obtaining any necessary authorizations from any third party owner for use of their lands.

12. **Scope Acknowledgment**: By signing this proposal, Client acknowledges and accepts that the Scope of Services provided by LTDS in this contract are dependent upon a number of factors, including but not limited to: weather conditions and any unforeseen circumstances arising during the course of the Scope of Services. Client agrees to accept any reasonable delays to completion of the Scope of Services due to matters beyond the control of the company.
