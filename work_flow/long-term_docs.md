# Long-Term Document Workflow

## Overview
Long-term documents are used to model ongoing relationships with a client — typically recurring services, memberships, or multi-invoice projects.

When a long-term Quote/Contract is created and approved, the system can schedule recurring invoices, track long-term contract state, and generate invoices automatically according to project settings.

## Typical elements
- Long-term Quote: An initial proposal which is then converted to a long-term Contract on approval.
- Long-term Contract: Contains the schedule and terms for recurring billing, or the on-demand invoicing rules.
- Recurring Invoices: Invoices generated according to the schedule defined on a long-term contract.

## States and Transitions

### Quotes
- `pending` → `approved` or `denied`
- When `approved`, a long-term contract and the first invoice are created.
	- The contract is usually `pending` until a signed PDF or activation is received.

### Contracts
- Statuses for long-term contracts are similar to regular contracts: `pending`, `active`, `complete`, etc.
- Long-term contracts may include schedule metadata in `project_meta` that controls interval and recurring invoice generation.

### Recurring / Scheduled Invoices
- Recurring invoices are generated by `cron` tasks (cron.dockerfile) or scheduled jobs.
- The `cron` jobs evaluate long-term contracts and create invoices as required by the schedule (e.g., monthly, weekly), then notify stakeholders.
- Reminders and overdue notifications are handled by the job scheduler.

## Cron tasks and scheduled behavior
- The `cron` tasks in `docker/cron.dockerfile` orchestrate periodic tasks like invoice generation, reminders, and status updates.
- Admins can control global behavior via the `settings` page. In settings, the user can:
	- Enable automatic invoice generation
	- Configure reminder days (e.g., 7-day pre-due reminder)
	- Toggle weekly reports

## Best practices
- Keep `project_code` consistent across the long-term contract and its invoices. This makes it easier to track long-term projects in reporting.
- Use `project_meta` to store schedule/notes that affect recurring invoice generation.
- When changing client or billing info, update active long-term contract entries to avoid incorrect recurring invoices.
